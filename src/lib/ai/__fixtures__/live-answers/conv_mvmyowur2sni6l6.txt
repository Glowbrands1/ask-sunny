**Cell phones:** Personal employee cell phones are permitted in the workplace but must be kept out of sight ("Cell Phone/Smart Watch Policy", JBA Policy Manual p.19 [p.19]). Phones should be on silent and not on the employee's person — not in back pockets, not charging in the break room, not sitting on a drawer till ("Cell Phone/Smart Watch Policy", JBA Policy Manual p.19 [p.19]). The only time a phone should be accessed while clocked in is in an emergency ("Cell Phone/Smart Watch Policy", JBA Policy Manual p.19 [p.19]). The reasoning given is that texting, calls and social media jeopardize the customer's experience, and protecting that experience is the employee's personal responsibility ("Cell Phone/Smart Watch Policy", JBA Policy Manual p.19 [p.19]).

**Smart watches:** Allowed, as long as they are not being used for social media, texting or phone calls, and the company reserves the right to ask an employee to remove one if it starts to interfere with the customer's experience ("Cell Phone/Smart Watch Policy", JBA Policy Manual p.19 [p.19]).

One related point: texting is not an acceptable form of communication for official work matters such as schedule changes, calling off, shift changes, request offs, availability changes, notice, complaints or disciplinary conversations — those require in person or a phone call ("Texting as General Work communications", JBA Policy Manual p.15 [p.15]).
