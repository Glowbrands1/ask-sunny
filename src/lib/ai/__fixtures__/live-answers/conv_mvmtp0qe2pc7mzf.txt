Here's the Sun Tan City salon dress code, straight from policy:

**Overall standard** — Employees must keep a neat, clean, professional appearance at all times. Anyone violating the dress code will be sent home to change into proper work attire and may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and potentially including termination [S1].

**Shirts**
- Any branded top, or a black collared shirt or black t-shirt. ASD and above may wear any colored collared shirt.
- Any gray, black, or white plain jacket.
- No shirts shorter than the beltline (exposing the stomach) or low cut to expose the chest.
- Clean, wrinkle-free, no patches, rips, or holes.

**Pants** [S4]
- Full length khaki or black pants.
- Full length black or blue jean-colored pants are acceptable as long as they are free of tears or excessive distressing.
- No yoga pants, joggers, or sweats — ever.
- Shorts or skirts that sit at or below the knee (Bermuda shorts, Capris) are acceptable.
- No noticeable patches, rips, or holes.

**Shoes** [S4]
- Tennis shoes or other full-coverage closed-toe shoes.
- No sandals, crocs, flip-flops, or open-toe shoes.
- Must be tied at all times, clean, no holes.

**Belts** [S4]
- Dark brown or black. Nothing hanging from them — no cell phones, pagers, chains, or keys.

**Name tags** [S1]
- Worn and visible at all times while working. Each employee gets one; additional or replacement tags are ordered through a manager at the employee's expense.

**Grooming and accessories**
- Hair: any length or color, as long as it is clean, groomed, and out of the face and eyes.
- Facial hair: clean, well-groomed, trimmed.
- Makeup: reasonable and natural makeup is acceptable and encouraged.
- Jewelry: anything that does not interfere with work. No dangling earrings or chains, no pins or buttons on the shirt unless approved by management.
- Tattoos: large, inappropriate, or offensive tattoos must be covered; others are fine as long as they are not discriminatory or offensive.

Note that all aspects of the dress code are at the Company's discretion — if an employee has a question, they should check with management before wearing it. District Managers and above follow a separate business casual standard [S5].
