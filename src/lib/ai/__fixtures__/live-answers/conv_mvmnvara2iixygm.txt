**What PTO is**
PTO is paid time away from work for vacation, personal time, personal illness, or caring for dependents, and it replaces sick, absence, Paid time Off (PTO), JBA Policy Manual p.41 [p.41] [p.1]).

**Who accrues it**
All salaried managers, full-time corporate office personnel, and full-time Assistant Salon Directors begin accruing PTO after 90 days of employment in that position. Eligibility, JBA Policy Manual p.41 [p.41] [p.1]).

**How much accrues**
- 90 days – 4 years of full-time service: 2 weeks (80 hours), accrual cap 120 hours [p.1]
- 5–9 years: 3 weeks (120 hours), accrual cap 160 hours [p.1]
- 10+ years: 4 weeks (160 hours), accrual cap 200 hours [p.42]

Unused PTO carries over year to year, but employees cannot exceed their tier's cap — once the cap is hit, Provisions, JBA Policy Manual p.42 [p.42] [p.1]).

**Using it**
- Salaried employees must schedule PTO in half-day (4 hour) or full-day (8 hour) increments ("Usage" Usage, JBA Policy Manual p.42 [p.42] [p.1]).
- No PTO during the Sun Tan City peak season listed as February 1 and June 1 [p.42].
- Request it in the Personnel System (MyGlow for Sun Tan City) at least two weeks in advance; if something unforeseen comes up, call your supervisor directly and enter the request as soon as possible [p.42].
- It must be approved by the immediate supervisor and will be granted as long as it doesn't place undue hardship on the business. Only one team member per location may be approved off at the same time unless the supervisor approves otherwise [p.42].
- Payout of accrued PTO at termination follows all state and federal guidelines [p.22].

One related note: bereavement days are entered under the PTO section of the personnel profile but do **not** deduct from the PTO balance ("Bereavement Leave" Bereavement Leave, JBA Policy Manual p.47 [p.47] [p.1]).
