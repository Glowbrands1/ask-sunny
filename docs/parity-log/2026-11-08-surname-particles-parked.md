# Both apps, parked: a surname particle cuts a typed name short ("maria de la cruz" → "maria de")

Found while fixing sf-s38 (8 Nov 2026); parked by QA the same day, because neither app's directory has an employee with a particle surname (Ask Sunny 0 of 164, Ask Bubbles 0 of 92). Not a live bug today.

**Cause.** `readTypedName` in the protected `forms/proposal.ts` (the same function in both apps) reads at most two words of a typed name for a whole message and three inside a sentence, a particle counts as one of them, a third word is dropped unless the name visibly ends, and the dot after "St." ends the name. So "maria de la cruz" / "Maria de la Cruz" read "maria de" / "Maria de", "pieter van der berg" reads "pieter van", "Mary St. James" reads "Mary St" ("Ana del Rio" already reads whole). Through the chat, an off-roster "make me a CA for ines de la reyna, she was late opening the salon on 11/2" makes the card for "Ines De"; a roster person resolves to the full name anyway.

**The parked fix** (behind a new SURNAME_PARTICLES_ENABLED, default off; it needs Pau's OK on these protected lines): `nameWordCount(parts)` in place of `parts.length` in the two length checks (`if (parts.length === (whole ? 2 : 3)) break;` and `if (parts.length === 3 && !endedOnPunctuation) {`, with `words[3]` becoming `words[parts.length]`), and "St." not ending the name when `isSurnameParticle(opened, words[index + 1])` (a name word follows it). The two helpers go in the unprotected `forms/name-words.ts`: the particle list (de, del, della, di, da, dos, das, du, la, le, van, von, der, den, ter, st, ste) is skipped by the count only with the flag on, and only before another name word. Measured with the flag on: the names above read whole; "Ana del Rio", "maria cruz", "rhea calloway", "for mary anne cruz to salon 24", "rosalyn co wore slippers" and "Leo de," are unchanged. The patch is kept by coding 4.

**Also parked:** "St." loses its dot after the reader ("Mary St James"); a roster person's particles are title-cased on the card and the saved form ("de la Vega" → "De La Vega"); a roster "<First> St. <Surname>" gets "Is this for …?" and no card.
