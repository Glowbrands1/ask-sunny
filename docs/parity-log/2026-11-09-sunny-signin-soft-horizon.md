## 9 Nov 2026: "soft horizon" sign-in screen — Sunny-only

The sign-in screen takes the "soft horizon" design: cream-to-peach panels, a
rising sun behind the right panel, a rayed sun lockup, Fraunces headings,
56px fields and a show/hide password toggle. Visual only: `SignInForm` still
sends the credential to Supabase Auth and nowhere else, and demo mode and the
"not configured" notice are unchanged. Not ported to Ask Bubbles: the design
is Sun Tan City's look (sun imagery, salon slogan), and Bubbles keeps its own
brand's sign-in screen. The password toggle is a small UX addition Bubbles can
take with its own redesign if wanted.
