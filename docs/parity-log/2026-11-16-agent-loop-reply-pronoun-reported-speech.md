# Agent loop: a pronoun in another person's reported question is the card person's (16 Nov 2026)

**Sibling app: not needed.** Ask Bubbles has no agent loop yet.

Pau's ruling via QA (Runner 2's c090 T5, c091 T2): in "When <M> asked whether she had completed her 10-minute
sanitization, <P> said yes.", "she" and "her" are <P>'s. Before this, the pronoun pass left any pronoun alone once another
person was named earlier in the sentence. Now, when the card's person is also named in the same sentence, a pronoun inside
the clause opened by the other person's reporting verb ("<M> asked / told / said / reminded … whether / if / that / to …",
up to the clause's comma) is replaced. Anything else after the other person's name stays as written: "<M> and her figures"
(c210 T2) and "<M> … during her onboarding" (c097). Port with the rest of `reply-pronouns.ts` when Ask Bubbles gets the
loop.
