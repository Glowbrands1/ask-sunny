# Not needed in Ask Bubbles: the Action Plan names only the card's policy (CARD_POLICY_PLAN_ONLY_ENABLED)

QA, 8 Nov 2026: an under-performance Corrective Action here ("put together a corrective action for <A> due to under
performance in salon metrics for the month of october") sometimes planned to "review the Standards of Conduct and
Evaluations and Raises policies" while its Policy line cited only Standards of Conduct. The plan's names came from
the reply's retrieved citations (`ai/keep-filled-slots.ts` withActionPlan), and a second section sometimes added
itself. CARD_POLICY_PLAN_ONLY_ENABLED (default off) keeps the plan, the citation lines and the citations to the
policy the card's Policy line names.

Not needed in Ask Bubbles: checked on its main 8 Nov 2026. Its card's Action plan is written in the proposal from
the policy the card names (`namedPolicy`), never from retrieved citations. With no named policy it reads "the
applicable company policy", so a second retrieved section cannot add itself to the plan.
